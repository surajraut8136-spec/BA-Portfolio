About

Business Analyst by craft. Product thinker by practice.

I have worked across digital eCommerce, Banking & Financial Services, Payments, SaaS, Compliance, AI-enabled platforms and enterprise applications. The common thread is solving complex business problems and making them executable for delivery teams.

My work usually starts before a user story exists. I work with business leaders, Product Owners, Operations, Compliance and technology teams to understand the actual problem, current process, desired outcome, constraints and decision points.

I then turn that understanding into structured requirements, AS-IS / TO-BE process models, Epics, Features, User Stories, acceptance criteria, risks, dependencies and release-ready decisions.

“A good requirement is not just documented. It is understood, testable, traceable and ready to move.”

AI in my BA workflow

Use AI to accelerate analysis—not replace judgment.

I use generative AI as a structured assistant for requirement synthesis, edge-case exploration, documentation, process comparison and stakeholder communication. Business context, validation and final decisions remain human-owned.

Discovery: consolidate meeting notes and identify recurring themes. Requirements: improve first drafts and surface missing scenarios. Process: compare current and target workflows for gaps and duplication. Testing: generate candidate positive, negative and boundary scenarios for validation. Communication: tailor the same decision for executives, business users and technical teams.
